Privacy Policy for Fullercode

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Fullercode Android application published under the package name com.fullercode.app.

Location information

Fullercode requests access to your approximate and precise location while the application is visible. Your location is used only on your device to calculate and display a Fullercode, coordinates, and location accuracy.

Fullercode does not access your location in the background.

Data collection and sharing

The Fullercode Android application does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Location information is not sent to Fullercode or to any third party, and it is not stored on a remote server.

The application does not contain advertising, analytics, user accounts, or tracking technologies.

Location permission

You may refuse the location permission or revoke it at any time in Android system settings. Without this permission, the application cannot calculate a Fullercode from your current position.

External website

The application contains a link to www.fullercode.org. The link opens in your device's web browser. This application privacy policy does not describe data processed independently by your browser or by websites you choose to visit.

Children's privacy

Fullercode is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the application's data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
